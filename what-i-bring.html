WHAT YOU GET · WHAT I BRING

Operating know-how, not a generic template pack.

I bring a working knowledge of the frameworks and models that keep revenue teams running, learned in operating roles. Rather than hand you someone else’s templates, I build each one around how your business sells and delivers. Your team keeps all of it.

Each sample below uses fictional data for an imaginary company, to show the shape of the deliverable. Yours is built from your own data. Downloadable templates are coming soon.

RevOps scorecard

A structured assessment of your data, process, systems and people, scored so you can track progress over time.

WHAT’S IN IT
  • Scores across data, process, systems and people
  • The evidence behind each score
  • The top gaps, ranked by revenue impact
  • Re-scored after the Build phase to show progress
BUILT DURING  Diagnose

Lead-to-CS funnel model

Stage-by-stage conversion and velocity, from first lead through to customer success, set up for how your company sells.

WHAT’S IN IT
  • Conversion and time in stage, from first lead to renewal
  • Where leads and deals stall or drop out
  • Cuts by source, segment and region
  • Baselines from your own history, so targets are realistic
BUILT DURING  Diagnose, refreshed during Run

Stage definitions and field standards

Stages, exit criteria and field rules written down for your revenue model, so every team works the same way.

WHAT’S IN IT
  • Entry and exit criteria for every stage
  • Shared definitions of MQL, SQL, pipeline and bookings
  • Required fields, and who owns each one
  • A one-page reference for the team
BUILT DURING  Build

Forecast cadence

Forecast categories, roll-ups and a meeting agenda your leadership can run every week.

WHAT’S IN IT
  • Forecast categories: commit, best case and pipeline
  • Roll-up from rep to manager to leadership
  • A weekly forecast call agenda
  • Forecast-versus-actual tracking
BUILT DURING  Build, then run weekly

Dashboards

WHAT’S IN IT
  • Leadership view: pipeline coverage, bookings and forecast

  • Built in your CRM or BI tool, not a separate system

Capacity and quota model

Quotas, territories and plans tested against real capacity before you roll them out.

  • Rep capacity and ramp assumptions
