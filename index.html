02 TYPICALLY 60–90 DAYS

Implement the priority fixes from the audit and funnel review: stages, definitions, routing, integrations and dashboards. Weekly working sessions, shipped in increments.

FAQ

Common questions.

How much of your time do we get each week?+

It depends on the phase. The build phase is the most hands-on; on a retainer we agree a set number of hours a week up front, plus your regular pipeline and forecast reviews.

Can we hire you for just one project?+

Yes. Project work has a fixed scope, a fixed fee and a clear handover, with no retainer. Typical examples are a CRM migration, a dashboard and forecast build, or territory and quota planning.

Which CRM and tools do you work with?+

Whatever you already use: Salesforce, HubSpot, Zoho and the tools around them. Recommendations are based on what fits your team, not on a vendor relationship, and the first answer is usually to get more out of what you’re already paying for.

We’re a services or IT company, not SaaS. Is this for us?+

Yes. Services and IT companies are a core focus, not an afterthought. Capacity planning, SOW workflows, partner pipelines and AMC renewals are built into how I approach those engagements.

What happens when we hire a full-time RevOps person?+

That’s a good outcome. I can help define the role, interview candidates and onboard them. Everything I build is documented, so they inherit a working system rather than starting over.

Do you work with teams outside India?+

Yes. Engagements run remotely, and working sessions are scheduled to overlap with your team’s hours.

Can we sign an NDA before sharing data?+

Yes. You can sign my Mutual NDA online in a couple of minutes, or upload your own NDA on the same page and I’ll review it.
